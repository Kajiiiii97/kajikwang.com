The Chandelion

context
SUTD AirLab for Chanel
year
2022
type
product
my role
3D printing and installation

The Chandelion is a 1.5 metre chandelier made for Chanel's sustainability programme, built almost entirely from things that would otherwise have been thrown away. 153 used perfume bottles burst out from a central hub like the seeds of a dandelion, each one sitting at the end of a slim tube made from upcycled aluminium cans.

The hub is a 3D printed dodecahedron in PLA, a bioplastic made from corn, designed so it prints without any support material and leaves no waste behind. New 3D printed caps clip each bottle onto its tube, so the original bottles are reused as they are. The piece can hang from the ceiling or sit on a stand and slowly turn, sending shifting light and reflections through the glass.

Designed and built at AirLab SUTD, it is a small argument for the circular economy: everyday objects given a second life through digital design and fabrication.
