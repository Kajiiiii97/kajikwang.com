Lamparda is a whimsical lightweight chandelier specially designed for the common room in a multigenerational family home. Made with a 300gsm paper and 2 6mm thick precision milled CNC rings, this chandelier uses no glue and only sewing between the papers and precision fit connections between the paper and the wood rings.
